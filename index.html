Recipe keeper, meal planner and grocery list in one

Plantry is a recipe keeper, meal planner and grocery list app for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch that takes the nightly “what’s for dinner?” question off your plate. Keep the recipes you love in one place, then tell Plantry how many meals you need, how much time you have and what to use up, and it fills your week with varied meals, your own recipes and favorites included, and writes the shopping list in seconds. Change the time or ingredients at the top of any plan, swap a meal with one tap, and get suggestions that use up what you have first.

Save recipes from any website, Instagram or TikTok, write down family favorites, or pick from more than 1,200 dinners by our chefs, from quick weeknight meals to weekend favorites. They all live in the same cookbook. Keep the week’s meal plan and shopping list on your Lock Screen or Home Screen with widgets, and cook with timers built right into every recipe.

Got half a fridge to use up? Type in what you have, or snap a few photos of your fridge, and the Leftovers flow ranks recipes by how much of it they use. Add to the shopping list by voice, and it sorts itself into supermarket aisles in the order you actually shop, gives every ingredient its own illustrated icon, shows each item’s climate impact, and prints beautifully alongside any recipe or plan.

How does Plantry plan my week?

Answer a few quick questions: how many meals you need, how much time you have and what you want to use up. Plantry fills the plan with varied meals, mixing our chefs’ recipes with your own and your favorites, and writes the shopping list for you. Every plan stays flexible: change the time or ingredients at the top, swap a meal with one tap, and suggestions put the recipes that use up what you already have first.

Does Plantry work on iPhone Duo?

Yes. Unfold it and Plantry fills the big display with more of your cookbooks, plans and recipes. The hinge does the work: fold it on a recipe and cooking mode takes over, or half-fold it sideways so the ingredients sit on one side and the steps on the other.

Does Plantry work on Apple Watch?

Yes. Your shopping list comes along on your wrist: check items off as you shop, add more by voice, and see the week’s meal plan at a glance. Changes go straight back to your iPhone.

Is Plantry free?

Plantry is free to download, and every new member gets a 7-day free trial. After that, an in-app subscription unlocks all meal plans and recipes. The current price is always shown in the app before you commit, and you can cancel anytime.

What kinds of recipes can I keep in Plantry?

Every kind. Save any recipe from the web or write your own, from fish, meat and poultry to vegan, and it lands in the same cookbook, the same meal plan and the same shopping list. Our chefs’ recipes include a big collection of vegetarian and vegan dishes, and Dietary Preferences shows only those whenever you like.

Can I see only vegetarian, vegan or gluten-free recipes?

Yes. Dietary Preferences in Settings filters the whole app: to vegetarian or vegan, to gluten-free, or to gluten-free together with either. Search, meal plans and suggestions all follow your choice.

Can I use Plantry as a recipe keeper?

Yes. Plantry doubles as your own digital cookbook. Save recipes from any website, add family favorites from scratch, or tweak ours to match how you cook. Everything you collect lives in My Cookbook, which you can fully customize with your own name, description and image, and every recipe there is ready to drop into next week’s meal plan.

Can I save recipes from Instagram and TikTok?

Yes. Share a cooking Reel or a TikTok to Plantry and it pulls in the video, reads the caption into a full recipe, and writes out the steps from the video when the caption leaves them out. The clip plays right at the top of the saved recipe, so you can follow along as you cook.

How does the grocery list work?

When you plan your week, Plantry automatically collects every ingredient you’ll need into one shopping list, so one trip to the store covers the whole week. Items sort themselves into supermarket aisles, and you can drag those aisles into the order you actually walk your store. Every ingredient gets a hand-drawn illustration, so the list is quick to scan with a trolley in one hand. Add and edit your own items by typing or by voice, with suggestions that follow your diet and learn what you usually buy.

Does Plantry show the climate impact of my food?

Yes. A dot by each item on your shopping list shows its climate impact: green for low, yellow for medium, red for high. Tap an item to see its greenhouse gas emissions per kilo, counted from the farm to the shop shelf, from CONCITO’s Big Climate Database. Rather shop without the dots? Turn off Show Climate Impact in Settings.

Can Plantry help me use up leftovers?

Yes. That’s what the Leftovers flow is for. Type in what you already have at home, or take a few photos of your fridge, and Plantry ranks recipes by how much of it they use. Pick the ones you like and they go straight into your plan. You can also tell the planner what to use up when you start a plan, and its suggestions put the recipes that use it first.

Can I cook without getting the screen dirty?

Yes. Full-screen cooking with hands-free reads each step aloud and takes voice commands, so you never have to tap the screen with sticky fingers. Hand gestures work too, and it listens in the language you use Plantry in, or in English if your iPhone can’t recognize that language.

Can I change how many people a recipe serves?

Set your household serving size once and every recipe scales to match, along with the amounts on your shopping list. Cooking for guests? Set a different number on any individual recipe without changing your default.

Are the recipes in cups or grams?

Whichever you cook in. Under Settings → Language, pick English (U.S.) for cups and ounces or English (Metric) for deciliters and grams. Plantry’s own recipes and the amounts on your shopping list follow your choice.

Can I print a recipe or my shopping list?

Yes. You can print a single recipe, a whole meal plan, or your shopping list, each laid out for paper rather than dumped from the screen.

What can I put on my Lock Screen?

Plantry has widgets for both your meal plan and your shopping list, on the Lock Screen and the Home Screen. Keep tonight’s recipe and how far through the week you are in view, or carry the list itself so it’s one glance away in the store. On iOS 27 both come in a new extra-large size, with more in view at a glance.

Does Plantry work on iPad?

Yes. Plantry is made for both iPhone and iPad, and Apple has featured it among the best apps for iPadOS.
