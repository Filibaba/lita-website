The plant-based meal planner for busy weeks

Plantry is a meal planner and grocery list app for iPhone and iPad that takes the nightly “what’s for dinner?” question off your plate. Pick a ready-made weekly meal plan, tweak it, or build your own from scratch — and Plantry writes the shopping list for you in seconds.

Every recipe is plant-based: more than 1,000 vegetarian and vegan dinners created by our chefs, from quick weeknight meals to weekend favorites. Save recipes you find on the web into the same collection, keep the week’s meal plan on your Lock Screen or Home Screen with widgets, and cook with timers built right into every recipe.

Is Plantry free?

Plantry is free to download, and every new member gets a 7-day free trial. After that, an in-app subscription unlocks all meal plans and recipes — the current price is always shown in the app before you commit, and you can cancel anytime.

Are the recipes vegetarian or vegan?

Everything in Plantry is plant-based — a mix of vegan and vegetarian dishes from chefs who cook this way every day.

Can I use my own recipes?

Yes. Turn any recipe from the web into a Plantry recipe, add your own from scratch, or tweak ours to match how you cook.

How does the grocery list work?

When you plan your week, Plantry automatically collects every ingredient you’ll need into one shopping list, so one trip to the store covers the whole week.

Does Plantry work on iPad?

Yes — Plantry is made for both iPhone and iPad, and Apple has featured it among the best apps for iPadOS.

Is Plantry available in Swedish?

Yes — the app is fully localized in Swedish, with recipes from well-loved Swedish chefs. Läs mer på svenska →

Is Plantry available on Android?

Plantry is an iPhone and iPad app. There is currently no Android version.
